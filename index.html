Ethnocentrism Inventory

22 quick statements about your culture and other cultures. Go with your first gut reaction. There are no right or wrong answers, and nobody sees your responses but you.

Two quick definitions

“My culture”
The group whose way of life feels like home to you: your country, ethnic group, religion, or community. Pick whichever one comes to mind first and keep that one in mind the whole way through.
“Values and customs”
What a group thinks is important (values) and the traditions and everyday habits it follows (customs), like food, holidays, manners, or how families work.

About this inventory. These items are the revised Generalized Ethnocentrism (GENE) Scale by James Neuliep and James McCroskey, scored the way the authors recommend. It’s a classroom reflection tool, not a diagnosis or a measure of whether someone is a good person.
